AER26
Motor Shield

A two-piece sheet metal safety enclosure that encases the high-voltage motor and primary drive components for the AER26 Formula Electric car.

Aztec Electric Racing
6061-T6 Aluminum
FSAE Compliant
01 Overview

The AER26 motor shield is a redesign of the previous year's enclosure, rebuilt to reduce bulk in the rear of the car while keeping full coverage of the powertrain. All design was done in SolidWorks Sheet Metal.

This shield is a critical safety structure that encases the high-voltage motor and its primary drive components, separating them from the driver and the rest of the chassis while adhering to FSAE rules.

03 Key Contributions
Diameter Reduction

Optimized geometry to create the smallest possible enclosure while maintaining required clearance. This minimized packaging volume and contributed to overall lower vehicle mass.

Chassis Integration

Designed new custom mounting brackets to securely fix the shield and motor to the improved geometry of the AER26 car.

Material Selection

1.0mm 6061-T6 Aluminum, the lightest compliant material available that meets FSAE safety requirements while prioritizing low mass.

Design Type

Two-piece bolted clamp design that wraps around the motor assembly and fastens securely to the chassis via custom brackets, providing full 360° component coverage.

04 Challenges & Solutions
Challenge, Tighter Packaging

Minimizing the shield's outer diameter to reduce bulk in the rear of the car while maintaining clearance between the motor and the shield.

Solution

Iteratively refined the CAD model to shrink the diameter while maintaining adequate clearance, achieving roughly a 50% reduction in envelope size over the previous design.

Challenge, New Chassis Geometry

The AER26 chassis changed from the previous year, so the existing shield and its mounts no longer fit the updated frame.

Solution

Designed custom mounting brackets matched to the new tube positions, securely locating both the shield and motor on the AER26 frame.
